# Rain is the sky emptying its pockets

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479):
two lines explaining rain to a 10-year-old — one concrete, surprising analogy,
then one honest sentence about what that analogy maps and what it leaves out.

> Rain is the sky emptying its pockets: clouds shake out all the water they have been carrying, drop by drop, until their pockets are empty.
>
> It maps the water falling out of clouds, but leaves out that the sky refills its pockets by drinking the puddles back up, while real pockets stay empty until someone fills them again.

`main.py` holds the two lines as a literal, checks them against the
competition's gate at run time (1–400 characters, exactly two lines, no
leading/trailing whitespace, no control characters), and writes them to
`/output/entry.txt`. No network, no input files.
